# Selected Research Papers

Eight papers are divided evenly between the two project members. The numbering
matches the literature review in the
[Project Update 1 Google Doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ftVG-AbGJSgVw6haw_vA45QzFVoAhYiBJDZokYMzw7U/edit?usp=sharing).

## Member 1

1. **Oh et al. (2018)** - *Automated diagnosis of arrhythmia using combination
   of CNN and LSTM techniques with variable length heart beats*

   - File: `efti_papers/01_Oh_et_al_2018_CNN_LSTM_Variable_Length_Heartbeats.pdf`
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compbiomed.2018.06.002
   - Copy supplied by the project group.

2. **Kachuee, Fazeli, and Sarrafzadeh (2018)** - *ECG Heartbeat
   Classification: A Deep Transferable Representation*

   - File: `efti_papers/02_Kachuee_et_al_2018_Deep_Transferable_Representation.pdf`
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/ICHI.2018.00092
   - Open manuscript: https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.00794

3. **Mousavi, Afghah, and Acharya (2019)** - *Inter- and Intra-Patient ECG
   Heartbeat Classification for Arrhythmia Detection: A Sequence to Sequence
   Deep Learning Approach*

   - File: `efti_papers/03_Mousavi_et_al_2019_Seq2Seq_Inter_Intra_Patient.pdf`
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/ICASSP.2019.8683140
   - Open manuscript: https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.07421

4. **Romdhane et al. (2020)** - *Electrocardiogram heartbeat classification
   based on a deep convolutional neural network and focal loss*

   - File: `efti_papers/04_Romdhane_et_al_2020_Deep_CNN_Focal_Loss.pdf`
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compbiomed.2020.103866
   - Copy supplied by the project group.
